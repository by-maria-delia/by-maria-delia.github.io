# Maria Delia. Domain Glossary

The ubiquitous language for the Maria Delia storefront. This file is a glossary only:
no implementation details. Terms are added as they are resolved during design.

## Terms

### Guardapolvo (Product)
A made-to-order school smock: the thing being sold. Has a name, price, description,
available sizes, and one or more photos. Only **available** guardapolvos are shown in
the storefront. Each guardapolvo corresponds to a recognizable cut/silhouette
(e.g. Bloque, Campana, Cintura, Globo, Mariposa, Princesa, Doll).

### Available
The flag that decides whether a guardapolvo appears in the storefront. An unavailable
guardapolvo exists in the catalog but is hidden from shoppers.

### Customizer
The flow where a shopper personalizes a chosen guardapolvo before buying, selecting
size, pocket style, and print style, which then composes a pre-filled WhatsApp message.
No checkout or payment happens on the site; the sale is closed over WhatsApp.

### Stamp (Estampado / Print)
A print/pattern option the shopper can choose for their guardapolvo inside the
Customizer. Presented as a gallery of images.

### Pocket (Bolsillo)
A pocket-style option the shopper can choose inside the Customizer. Presented as a
gallery of images.

### Gallery
A collection of real photos of finished guardapolvos / customers, shown on the site as
social proof / inspiration. Distinct from the per-guardapolvo product photos.

### Editor
The non-technical person (the shop owner) who updates guardapolvo prices, texts, and
photos. Edits content without touching code or running a terminal.
